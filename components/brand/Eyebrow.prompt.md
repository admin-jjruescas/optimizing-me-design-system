**Eyebrow** — a small uppercase, wide-tracked label placed above section headings. Echoes the tracked "JJ RUESCAS" logotype.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>The HumanOps Framework</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow color="var(--brand-periwinkle)" showDot={false}>Breathwork</Eyebrow>
```

- Defaults to brand orange with a leading dot. Override `color`/`dotColor` per background.
