**IconButton** — circular icon-only button. Always pass `label` for accessibility and an outline SVG glyph as children.

```jsx
<IconButton label="Play breathwork" variant="accent"><PlayIcon/></IconButton>
<IconButton label="Menu" variant="soft"><MenuIcon/></IconButton>
```

- Variants: `soft` (periwinkle tint, default), `solid` (navy), `accent` (orange), `ghost`, `on-dark`.
