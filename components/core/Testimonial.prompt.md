**Testimonial** — a quote card with a large orange quotation mark, the quote, and attribution (name, role, optional avatar).

```jsx
<Testimonial
  quote="Breathwork rewired how I handle stress at work."
  name="Ana M." role="Product Lead" avatarSrc="…" tone="light" />
```

- `tone="dark"` for navy sections. Matches Quote Card template pattern 4.
