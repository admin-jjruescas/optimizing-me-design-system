The Problem

Something Is Off. And It's Not Your Imagination.

You started using AI to get more done. And it worked — for a while.

Then the scattered thinking started. The nighttime hyperarousal. The inability to stop switching between tabs, agents, and prompts. The vague sense that something is off, even though your output has never been higher.

Here's what's actually happening:

AI is an intelligence that never sleeps, operating at a constant PhD-level output. Your human operating system has hard processing limits. Every AI interaction — every prompt sent, every output evaluated, every decision made on AI-generated content — consumes processing power and memory that your system wasn't designed to sustain at this pace.

The result is what I call system overload. You're running processes that exceed your system's capacity, creating errors that compound over time.

Most AI courses teach you to do more, faster. None of them address what happens when your human system can't keep up. This one does.

Is This Right For You?

Find Your Fit

This Is For You If…

  • You're using ChatGPT but know there's more to AI and you're not sure where to start
  • You want to use AI but anxiety, privacy fears, or overwhelm keep getting in the way
  • You've noticed you're more scattered, more wired, or sleeping worse since increasing your AI use

This Is NOT For You If…
